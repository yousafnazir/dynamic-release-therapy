Meet the Innovator

Rooted in international manual therapy. Built for longevity.

Gary Lang began working with soft tissue instruments in the 1980s alongside pioneering hands-on clinicians in Northern California, Switzerland, Norway, and Australia. After testing wood, ceramic, jade, polymer, and early metal tools across Olympic and FIFA events, he engineered Dynamic Release Therapy to eliminate bruising and protect clinicians’ hands.
